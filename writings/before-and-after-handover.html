Self-Governance

Nov 12, 2026

Across the world

from guillotined monarchies,

rose heads of representation.

As more hands signed the contract,

good or bad,

fate was tied to the many.

Every vote in history had borders.

Every one could be tried again.

But now before us— stands a decision

heavier than all the hands ever raised.

It will reach every shore

and the stars past them.

It may be the last ballot we ever cast.

Will we leave it to the few?

Sibling Rivalry

Nov 13, 2026

I’ve always been the older brother.

All that we shared was mine.

Now, so late in life:

It seems I will be getting an older brother,

along with all of humanity.

Will you share with me?
